## What Worked
- STT service tests (fake fetch): text trimmed/capped, empty = not a failure, garbage/HTTP/network/timeout = null, no key = no request, tiny/huge/empty clips refused without a request, key only in a header, form carries model + audio file.
- Voice input tests (fake stream/recorder/clock): mic released after every clip, tap filtered, 10 s cap, double start ignored, released-before-mic-opened cancels cleanly, a blocked mic is reported once and works again later, 3,000-step fuzz ends with no mic open.
